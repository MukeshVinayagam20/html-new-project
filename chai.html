Masala Chai

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp tea leaves
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Spices: ginger,cardamom,cloves

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a pan.
  2. Add the spices:
    • Crush the ginger
    • Pound 2 cardamom pods
    • Add 2 cloves
  3. Add tea leaves and boil for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in milk and sugar, then simmer.
  5. Strain and serve hot.

Kitchen Terms

Simmer
Cook on low heat just below boiling
Strain
Pour through a sieve to remove solids.
Pound
Crush with a mortar and pestle

Top 3 Tips

  1. Use fresh ginger.
  2. Do not over-boil the milk
  3. Let the tea rest for 1 minute before straining.
